FM-1 Lyric Chords

The line

The line keys: — (E5) holds the last vowel over another note, _ (F5) leaves a note without words, × (F#5) deletes, ▶ (G5) sings the line. Click a syllable to hear it again.

Playing

In Sing mode each key or MIDI note sings the next syllable of the line at its own pitch, as a clip's notes do in Acidulous.

The chords

A cluster is its sounds' keys together: STR is S T R, PL is P HR, ND is PB D. A last S after P, T, K, F or TH goes on -Z (gets, six); a Z before D goes on -S (used). A Y after a consonant goes with the vowel: @AOU is "yoo", so "you" is just @AOU. The full table is in fm1/CHORDS.md.
