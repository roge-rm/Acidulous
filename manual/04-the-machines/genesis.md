# Genesis

> The big drum box, with a bus compressor the kick ducks.

Genesis is the big drum machine, in the style of the boxes house and techno
were made on. It has twelve voices, more weight than Hexbeat and two extras.

## Drift

Real analogue drum machines are never exactly the same twice, and **drift**
adds a bit of that variation from hit to hit.

## The bus compressor

The whole kit goes through a compressor (**comp**, **compattack**,
**comprelease**), and **duck** lets the kick push the rest of the kit down, so
the kit pumps with the kick.

## The kit

- **kick** - tune, decay, level, **sweep** (the long pitch drop), **punch**,
  **click** (the attack) and **drive**.
- **snare**, **clap** (with spread and tone) and **rim**.
- **toms**, and **hats** with shared tone and tune and separate open and closed
  decays.
- **crash** and **ride**, with a **bell** on the ride.
- **cowbell**.

## Macros

The **play** page has eight macro knobs. A patch can name them (Tighter, Room,
etc.) and the names show on the knobs, on automation lanes and in the
controller mapping list. They sit at zero when a patch loads, which is the
patch as it was made.

The **macros** page sets what each one does. A macro has four routes, a
destination (**to**) and an amount (**by**, from -1 to 1) for each. Turning the
knob moves the destination by knob times amount, across that control's whole
range at 1. Pitch moves every tune together, 24 semitones at 1. A tune or decay
change is heard from the next hit. Tap a macro's name to rename it.

## Tips

- Set **duck** before the levels, since it changes how loud everything else
  sounds.
- If the kick disappears on a phone speaker, add kick **drive** instead of
  level.
- Keep drift low, enough that bars differ but not so much that the hats sound
  broken.
