# Diction

> A voice that sings the notes, as a man, a woman or neither.

Diction sings. It's one singer, so it plays one note at a time, and it
slides from note to note while you hold them. For now it sings vowels. Words
come later.

## The voice

- **vowel** - oo, oh, ah, eh or ee, from the back of the mouth to the front.
  In between, it sings the vowel between them, so turning it moves through
  the vowels.
- **formant** - the size of the throat, without changing the pitch. Down is
  bigger and darker, up is smaller and brighter. Low notes with the formant
  down sound like a man, high notes with it up like a woman or a child, and
  in between is neither.
- **breath** - how much air is in the voice.

## Expression

- **vibrato** - **depth**, **rate** and **delay**. A singer holds a note
  straight for a moment before the vibrato comes in, and **delay** is how long.
  The mod wheel adds more.
- **drift** - how unsteady the voice is. It wanders a little on a held note
  and scoops up into a new one.
- **glide** - how long it takes to slide from one note to the next.

Then **attack**, **release** and **velocity**, the tuning, and the level.

## Tips

- Hold one note while you play the next and it slides there without starting
  again. Let go first and it sings the new note fresh.
- Let go of a note while holding another and it goes back to the one still
  held.
- For a different vowel on each note, lock **vowel** on the steps.
