# Hexbeat

> Synthesized drums in the style of the classic small boxes, with a bigger kit.

Hexbeat makes its drums with synthesis instead of samples. It has thirteen
voices in the style of the small classic drum machines, punchy, dry and easy to
sit in a mix.

## The kit

Kick, rim, snare, clap, three toms, closed and open hat, crash, ride, cowbell
and clave.

## The controls

Every voice has a **level**, and most have **tune** and **decay**, plus a few of
their own:

- **kick** - tune, decay and **punch** (the pitch drop at the start).
- **snare** - tune, decay, tone and **snappy** (drum against noise).
- **hats** - shared tone and tune, and separate closed and open decays.
- **clap** - decay, tone and the spread of the little repeats that make it a
  clap.
- **cymbals** - decay and tone for crash and ride.
- **bell** and **clave** - tune and level.

**accent** is how much a hard hit adds across the whole kit, and **vel** is how
much velocity sets each hit's level. **drive** is how hard the voices push the
output clipper: low is clean, high crunches a busy kit.

## Macros

The **macros** card on the kit page has eight macro knobs. A patch can name them
(Tighter, Room, etc.) and the names show on the knobs, on automation lanes
and in the controller mapping list. They sit at zero when a patch loads, which
is the patch as it was made.

The **macros** section sets what each one does. A macro has four routes, a
destination (**to**) and an amount (**by**, from -1 to 1) for each. Turning the
knob moves the destination by knob times amount, across that control's whole
range at 1. Pitch moves every tune together, 24 semitones at 1. A tune or decay
change is heard from the next hit. Tap a macro's name to rename it.

## Tips

- It opens on the drum grid, which is the best way to edit it.
- Changing the decays is a quick way to make a pattern tighter or roomier.
- Try probability on the hats and a ratchet on the snare so the loop doesn't
  repeat exactly.
- For drums from your own samples see Forage, and for a bigger, heavier kit
  see Genesis.
