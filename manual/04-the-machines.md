# The machines

> Nineteen instruments, a four-track for your recordings, and what each is for.

A track's machine is what it plays. Every track has one, chosen when you make the track and changed at any time from the name at the top of the editor. **Each machine has a page of its own below** with what it does and how to get the best from it; this page is for finding the one you want.

## Bass and lead

- **Reflux** - acid bass. One oscillator, one filter that screams, accent on velocity and slide on legato, so a line is played rather than programmed.
- **Trinity** - the general-purpose poly. Three wavetable oscillators, density and drift, two filters and a mod matrix.
- **Ratio** - six-operator FM, with the algorithm itself on a knob so it can morph between two of them.

## Keys and air

- **Cumulus** - pads by spectrum. You describe the sound's shape and the machine builds it, which is why it sounds like nothing else here.
- **Manual** - a tonewheel organ: two manuals and pedals over one shared generator, four models, and a cabinet that spins.
- **Formulate** - the 8-bit machine: chip oscillators, tracker tables, and a waveform you can type in as an equation.

## Modelled instruments

Not recordings and not filtered noise - models of how the real thing works, so they behave like it under the hands.

- **Filament** - strings, plucked, bowed or blown at, with a body and sympathetic strings.
- **Brazen** - brass: lips blown open against a tube, from a trumpet to a tuba on one control.
- **Timber** - woodwinds: a reed or a jet of air, a tube, and the holes down the side of it.
- **Resonance** - eight struck objects that ring, and hear each other.

## Drums

- **Hexbeat** - drums by synthesis in the small-box vocabulary, grown to the kit those boxes never had.
- **Genesis** - the big box: a swept kick, circuit drift, and a bus the kick ducks.
- **Forage** - the sample drum machine. Thirteen pads, your own files, with a filter and an envelope on each.
- **Dice** - a loop cut into pieces and rolled, with a probability on every trigger.

## Sound as material

- **Pollen** - granular clouds that seed their own, from a file or from what is coming in live.
- **Mosaic** - multisamples: zones across the keyboard and velocity, SoundFonts, and a grain engine over the map.
- **Molt** - a voice you write for. Sing a take in and the piano roll tunes it.
- **Cipher** - a vocoder whose band map is the instrument.

## Anything else

- **Nexus** - a modular whose blocks are this app's own instruments and whose patch is text.

## Audio

- **Bias** - the four-track. Four lanes of recordings per cell, sounding together; record over the song and the take is cut at the scene lines. Its patches are **recording media** rather than sounds - a cassette, a Portastudio, a reel, a MiniDisc, a telephone - which colour what comes out and never the recordings.

## Patches

Every machine ships with patches, shelved by family. **patch** at the top of the panel opens the browser; your own saved patches get a tab of their own and are the only ones you can delete.
