# The machines

> Nineteen instruments, a four-track for your recordings, and what each is for.

A track's machine is what it plays. Every track has one, chosen when you make the track and changed at any time from the name at the top of the editor. **Each machine has a page of its own below** with what it does and how to get the best from it; this page is for finding the one you want.

## Bass and lead

- [**Reflux**](04-the-machines/reflux.md) - acid bass. One oscillator, one filter that screams, accent on velocity and slide on legato, so a line is played rather than programmed.
- [**Trinity**](04-the-machines/trinity.md) - the general-purpose poly. Three wavetable oscillators, density and drift, two filters and a mod matrix.
- [**Ratio**](04-the-machines/ratio.md) - six-operator FM, with the algorithm itself on a knob so it can morph between two of them.

## Keys and air

- [**Cumulus**](04-the-machines/cumulus.md) - pads by spectrum. You describe the sound's shape and the machine builds it, which is why it sounds like nothing else here.
- [**Manual**](04-the-machines/manual.md) - a tonewheel organ: two manuals and pedals over one shared generator, four models, and a cabinet that spins.
- [**Formulate**](04-the-machines/formulate.md) - the 8-bit machine: chip oscillators, tracker tables, and a waveform you can type in as an equation.

## Modelled instruments

Not recordings and not filtered noise - models of how the real thing works, so they behave like it under the hands.

- [**Filament**](04-the-machines/filament.md) - strings, plucked, bowed or blown at, with a body and sympathetic strings.
- [**Brazen**](04-the-machines/brazen.md) - brass: lips blown open against a tube, from a trumpet to a tuba on one control.
- [**Timber**](04-the-machines/timber.md) - woodwinds: a reed or a jet of air, a tube, and the holes down the side of it.
- [**Resonance**](04-the-machines/resonance.md) - eight struck objects that ring, and hear each other.

## Drums

- [**Hexbeat**](04-the-machines/hexbeat.md) - drums by synthesis in the small-box vocabulary, grown to the kit those boxes never had.
- [**Genesis**](04-the-machines/genesis.md) - the big box: a swept kick, circuit drift, and a bus the kick ducks.
- [**Forage**](04-the-machines/forage.md) - the sample drum machine. Thirteen pads, your own files, with a filter and an envelope on each.
- [**Dice**](04-the-machines/dice.md) - a loop cut into pieces and rolled, with a probability on every trigger.

## Sound as material

- [**Pollen**](04-the-machines/pollen.md) - granular clouds that seed their own, from a file or from what is coming in live.
- [**Mosaic**](04-the-machines/mosaic.md) - multisamples: zones across the keyboard and velocity, SoundFonts, and a grain engine over the map.
- [**Molt**](04-the-machines/molt.md) - a voice you write for. Sing a take in and the piano roll tunes it.
- [**Cipher**](04-the-machines/cipher.md) - a vocoder whose band map is the instrument.

## Anything else

- [**Nexus**](04-the-machines/nexus.md) - a modular whose blocks are this app's own instruments and whose patch is text.

## Audio

- [**Bias**](04-the-machines/bias.md) - the four-track. Four lanes of recordings per cell, sounding together; record over the song and the take is cut at the scene lines. Its patches are **recording media** rather than sounds - a cassette, a Portastudio, a reel, a MiniDisc, a telephone - which colour what comes out and never the recordings.

## Patches

Every machine ships with patches, shelved by family. **patch** at the top of the panel opens the browser; your own saved patches get a tab of their own and are the only ones you can delete.
