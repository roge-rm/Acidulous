# Mouth
> Diction's throat on any track: it shapes whatever goes through into a vowel.

## The controls

- **vowel** - oo, oh, ah, eh, ee from bottom to top.
- **size** - the throat's size, big and dark to small and bright.
- **nasal** - opens the nose.
- **move** *(extra)* - what moves the vowel: **lfo** on the tempo, the track's
  own **level** (louder opens the mouth), or another track through the
  **sidechain** (**key**).
- **rate** - the LFO's note value.
- **depth** - how far the vowel moves from where it's set.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the vowel.
- **gain** - the level out.
- **sidechain** - the track that moves it when **move** is **key**.

## Tips

- On a synth lead with **move** at **level**, harder notes say "ah".
- Point the sidechain at a drum loop and a pad talks in its rhythm.
- Slow **lfo** sweeps from oo to ah sound like a wah played by a voice.
