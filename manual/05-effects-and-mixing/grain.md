# Grain
> A cloud of short slices of what the track just played.

## The controls

- **size** - how long each grain is, 5 to 500 ms.
- **density** - how many grains start a second, 1 to 100.
- **spray** - how far back into the last four seconds they can reach. At 0
  they follow the part closely, at 1 they come from anywhere.
- **pitch** - what pitch they play at, two octaves either way.
- **scatter** - the chance a grain jumps an octave up or down.
- **reverse** - the chance a grain plays backwards.
- **freeze** *(extra)* - stops listening, so the cloud keeps playing what it
  has. A switch to play live.
- **feedback** *(extra)* - feeds the cloud back into what it listens to, so it
  grows on itself.
- **width** - how far apart the grains are spread across the stereo field.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the cloud.
- **gain** - the level out.

## Tips

- Map **freeze** to a button and catch the end of a phrase to hold under the
  next one.
- Long grains with a little **spray** blur a part. Short ones with a lot make
  a swarm.
- **feedback** with **pitch** at +7 makes a cloud that climbs in fifths.
