# Slicer
> The track cut on the tempo, some slices repeated, reversed or dropped.

## The controls

- **rate** - the slice: a quarter, eighth, sixteenth or thirty-second note.
- **chance** - how likely a slice is to change.
- **repeat**, **reverse**, **drop** - the proportions of what a changed slice
  does: repeat the one before it, play it backwards, or go silent.
- **gate** - shortens every slice.
- **pitch** *(extra)* - shifts the repeats, an octave either way.
- **seed** *(extra)* - which slices change. The same slices change every time
  the song plays, so a part can be written around them.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the sliced one.
- **gain** - the level out.

## Tips

- On a drum loop, **Stutter** adds fills you didn't program.
- Try seeds until a bar sounds right; it'll stay that way.
- With **chance** at 0, **gate** alone chops a pad in time.
