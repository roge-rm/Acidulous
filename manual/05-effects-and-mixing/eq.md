# Eq
> Three bands and a tilt.

A low shelf, a sweepable mid band and a high shelf.

## The controls

- **lowgain** and **lowfreq** - a shelf up to 15 dB either way, from 40 to
  500 Hz.
- **midgain**, **midfreq** and **midq** - the mid band. **midq** goes from broad
  (0.3) to very narrow (6).
- **highgain** and **highfreq** - the high shelf, from 1.5 to 16 kHz.
- **tilt** *(extra)* - one knob that lifts the top and lowers the bottom, or the
  other way round.
- **gain** - output level.

## Tips

- Cutting usually helps more than boosting.
- To get rid of a ringing frequency, boost with a narrow Q, sweep until it's
  worst, then cut. For changing the tone, use a broad Q and small moves.
- Try **tilt** first when something sounds too dark or too thin.
