# Resonator
> Strings tuned to a key, ringing in sympathy with the track.

## The controls

- **key** and **scale** - the strings' key. Set them to the song's.
- **low** - the lowest string, as a note. The strings climb the scale from the
  first note of the key at or above it.
- **strings** - how many, 4 to 16.
- **decay** - how long they ring, 0.2 to 12 s.
- **tone** - how bright they are.
- **metal** *(extra)* - stiffness in the strings, which pulls their overtones
  sharp, from a string toward a bell.
- **width** - how far apart low and high strings sit in the stereo.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the strings.
- **gain** - the level out.

A note in the key sets its strings ringing. A note out of it mostly doesn't,
so a part in the key blooms like the open strings of a sitar or a piano with
its dampers up.

## Tips

- On drums, it gives every hit a pitch in the key.
- A long **decay** with a low **low** turns a part into a drone under it.
- Put it on a send, so several tracks set the same strings ringing.
