# Midside
> The middle and the sides, each with its own level and tilt.

Splits the sound into what both sides share (the middle) and how they differ
(the sides), works on each, and puts them back. A mono track has no sides,
so only the middle's controls act on it.

## The controls

- **width** - 0 is mono, 1 is unchanged and 2 is twice as wide.
- **mid** - the middle's level.
- **side** - the sides' level.
- **midtilt** - leans the middle brighter or darker, around 650 Hz.
- **sidetilt** - the same for the sides.
- **mono** - takes the sides out below this frequency, so the bass sits in
  the middle. At the bottom of its range it's off.
- **breathe** *(extra)* - the sides drop while the middle is loud and open up
  in the gaps, so a part goes narrow when it plays and wide as it rings.
- **gain** - the output level.

## Tips

- **sidetilt** up with a little more **width** opens the top of a mix without
  making the bass wider.
- **side** down and **mid** up puts a lead in front of everything else.
- **breathe** on a pad or a reverb return keeps the middle clear for the part
  on top.
