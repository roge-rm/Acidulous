# Venue
> Puts the track in a room, from a recording of how the room answers.

Venue plays the track through an impulse: a short recording of a room
answering a single click. Every echo and every reflection in it lands on the
track the same way, so the track sounds as if it were played there.

It comes with five rooms of our own: a small room, a wooden hall, a stone
church, a metal plate and a closet. A new Venue starts in the wooden hall.

## The impulse

Tap the **room** button to choose one. Our rooms are listed under **rooms**,
and any you've brought in under **yours**. **none** lets the track through
untouched.

**open…** brings in a WAV, FLAC or AIFF file as an impulse. It's copied into
your impulses, up to 20 seconds of it, and goes in a song's zip when you
share the song, so it plays on the other device too.

## The controls

- **predelay** - waits up to 250 ms before the room answers, which puts the
  sound further forward.
- **length** - how much of the impulse is used, 0.1 to 10 seconds. The end
  is faded out. Shorter is lighter on the phone.
- **decay** - fades the impulse out sooner, as if the room were deader. At
  the top it's off and the impulse rings as recorded.
- **stretch** *(extra)* - plays the impulse slower or faster, half to twice
  as long: a bigger or smaller room of the same shape.
- **lowcut**, **highcut** - take lows and highs out of the room, not out of
  the track.
- **width** - how wide the room sounds, from mono to wider than it was.
- **mix** - the dry track against the room.
- **gain** - the level out.

**length**, **decay** and **stretch** reshape the impulse, so a turn of one
takes a moment to be heard. They can't be drawn as lanes or moved by the
**mod** card.

## Tips

- As a send, with **mix** all the way up, one Venue can be the room for the
  whole song.
- The closet and the small room on a drum track add body without sounding
  like a reverb.
