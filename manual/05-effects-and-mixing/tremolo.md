# Tremolo
> Volume on an LFO, or auto-pan.

## The controls

- **rate** - the pulse speed, as a note value.
- **depth** - how deep each dip goes. At full, the sound cuts out between
  pulses.
- **shape** - sine, triangle or square. Square gives a chopped sound.
- **pan** *(extra)* - moves the two channels out of step. At zero it's a
  tremolo, at full it's an auto-pan, and in between it wobbles around.
- **skew** *(extra)* - changes the shape of each pulse: a stab (fast down, slow
  up) or a swell (slow down, fast up).
- **mix** - wet against dry. On a tremolo this effectively sets the depth.

## Tips

- Square shape with **skew** hard over at a sixteenth rate turns a held chord
  into a rhythm.
- Try **pan** around a third of the way for movement that never sits clearly on
  one side.
