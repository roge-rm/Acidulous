# Phaser
> Two to eight stages.

Sweeping notches from a chain of allpass filters. Unlike a flanger's, the
notches aren't evenly spaced, which gives a phaser its own sound.

## The controls

- **rate** - the sweep speed, as a note value.
- **depth** - how far the notches travel.
- **feedback** - up to 0.9. Makes the notches ring.
- **stages** - two, four, six or eight. More stages means more notches and a
  stronger effect.
- **spread** *(extra)* - sweeps the two channels out of step, so it's wide.
  Great on pads.
- **mix** - wet against dry.

## Tips

- Change **stages** first to set the character. Four is the classic pedal.
- More feedback makes the sweep more obvious.
- A slow phaser on a send adds movement without being noticeable.
