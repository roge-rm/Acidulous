# Chorus
> Two to four detuned voices that drift.

A chorus made of several voices at different delays, each swept by its own part
of the LFO, so it sounds like a group rather than one doubled copy.

## The controls

- **rate** - how fast the voices sweep, as a note value.
- **depth** - how far each one moves. Small thickens; large gets seasick.
- **voices** - two, three or four. Two sounds like doubling, four like an
  ensemble.
- **spread** - how wide the voices are in stereo.
- **drift** *(extra)* - each voice wanders randomly, like an old analogue chorus
  with an unsteady clock.
- **mix** - wet against dry.

## Tips

- If it sounds fake, use more voices and less depth.
- A little **drift** makes it sound analogue.
- It's wide by default. On a lead or bass, turn **spread** down.
