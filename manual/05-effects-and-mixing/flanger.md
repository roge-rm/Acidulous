# Flanger
> A short sweeping delay, with negative feedback for the hollow sound.

One very short delay, swept and mixed back with the dry sound.

## The controls

- **rate** - the sweep speed, as a note value.
- **depth** - how far the sweep travels.
- **feedback** - up to 0.95. Low is a gentle whoosh, high is the jet plane.
- **negative** *(extra)* - flips the feedback, for a hollow sound instead of a
  ringing one.
- **spread** - how far out of step the two channels sweep.
- **mix** - wet against dry. Half is the strongest effect.

## Tips

- Keep the mix around half. Fully wet loses most of the effect.
- Try **negative** on sustained chords.
- A slow rate and small depth on a send adds subtle movement to a mix.
