# Width
> Wider, narrower, mono below a frequency, or rotated.

Controls the stereo image.

## The controls

- **width** - 0 is mono, 1 is unchanged, 2 is twice as wide. Anything in the
  centre stays in the centre.
- **below** *(extra)* - makes everything under this frequency mono, from 20 to
  500 Hz.
- **haas** *(extra)* - delays one side by up to 20 ms. A few milliseconds makes
  a sound wider without changing its tone.
- **rotate** - turns the whole image left or right without changing its width.

## Tips

- Set **below** to around 120 Hz on most things. Bass doesn't need stereo, and
  stereo bass can vanish on mono speakers.
- **haas** holds up better than **width** when the mix is played in mono.
