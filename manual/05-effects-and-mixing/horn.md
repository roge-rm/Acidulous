# Horn
> The track plays a wind instrument.

Horn listens for the pitch of the track and how loud it is, and plays the
same line on a horn, a clarinet, an oboe or a flute: the level becomes the
breath. It follows one note at a time, so give it a lead, a bass line or a
voice. Sing into an input track and you play a trumpet.

## The controls

- **kind** - **brass**, **clarinet** (a single reed), **oboe** (a double
  reed) or **flute** (an air jet).
- **octave** - plays up to two octaves away from the track.
- **tone** - the lips' bite on the brass, or how stiff the reed is.
- **bell** - how bright the bell lets the sound out.
- **air** - breath noise.
- **glide** - how fast it moves to a new note, 1 to 500 ms.
- **breath** *(extra)* - how hard a given level blows it. Turn it up for a
  quiet track.
- **snap** *(extra)* - **free** follows the pitch as it is, slides and all;
  **semi** lands every note on a semitone.
- **mix** - the dry track against the instrument.
- **gain** - the level out.

## Tips

- **Low Brass** under a bass line doubles it with a tuba-like horn.
- **Sung Brass** on a sung part adds a horn that follows the singer.
- If it wobbles between notes, raise **glide** or switch **snap** to
  **semi**.
