# Tape
> A worn tape machine, and a stop button.

## The controls

- **wow** - slow wandering in the speed, and so the pitch.
- **flutter** - fast wobble in the speed.
- **drive** - saturation, as tape driven hard.
- **tone** - where the highs roll off, 2 to 20 kHz.
- **hiss** - the noise underneath.
- **age** - how worn: the wow wanders less evenly and the low end bumps up.
- **stop** *(extra)* - slows the tape to a halt, the pitch falling with it.
  Switched off, it's back at speed at once.
- **stoptime** *(extra)* - how long the stop takes, 0.1 to 4 s.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the tape.
- **gain** - the level out.

## Tips

- Map **stop** to a button and use it at the end of a phrase.
- **Cassette** on a whole mix makes it sound like a tape found in a drawer.
- **Seasick** on keys and pads makes them drift out of tune.
