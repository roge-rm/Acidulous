# Rotary
> The organ's rotating speaker cabinet, for any track.

## The controls

- **speed** - brake, slow or fast.
- **slow** - how fast the horn turns at slow, 0.1 to 2 Hz.
- **fast** - how fast it turns at fast, 2 to 10 Hz. The drum turns a little
  slower than the horn at both.
- **ramp** - how long the rotors take to reach a new speed. Slowing down
  takes nearly twice as long, as it does with the real motors.
- **distance** - how far the microphones are from the cabinet. Close is a
  deep swing, far a gentle one.
- **angle** - how far apart the two microphones point.
- **width** - how much of the stereo the microphones give.
- **tempo** *(extra)* - turns the horn once a note value (1/1 to 1/8 triplet)
  instead of at its own speed, so the swirl sits on the beat.
- **mix** - the dry sound against the cabinet.
- **gain** - the level out.

The cabinet has one input, so a stereo track is summed before it, as an
organ's would be.

## Tips

- Automate **speed** between slow and fast and let **ramp** do the rest.
- On a guitar or electric piano, **mix** at about 0.6 keeps the attack clear.
- With **tempo** on, a pad pulses in time with no LFO to set up.
